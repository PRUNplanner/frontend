This guide takes you from "I want to make this material" to a short list of planets and a new [[plan]] on the best one. It's for anyone choosing where to put a base, and it takes about 10 minutes. [Planet Search](/search) works without an account; you only log in for distances to your own plans and to create the plan.

## 1. Pick the material {#step-1}

Open [Planet Search](/search). Under Materials, click **+ add material…** and pick the resource you want to [[extraction|extract]]. Each option shows how many planets have it.

- Type a minimum in "min … /day" to hide planets that give too little. The "(max …)" next to it is the best any planet gives, so you know what's possible.
- Need two resources on one planet? Add both to one group and switch it to **all of**. With **any of**, either one is enough.

What good looks like: the heading reads "n of total planets" with a list you could still scan by eye.

More: [Materials](/help/planet_search#materials).

## 2. Set the planet conditions {#step-2}

- **Rocky** and **Gaseous** pick the surface; a new search starts with Rocky only. Tick **Fertile** when you want to farm.
- Under **Accept extra building materials**, tick the ones you're willing to ship in. Planets that need any other are hidden. Each one makes every building on the planet cost more, see [[extra-building-materials|extra building materials]].
- **Active COGC program** and **Infrastructure** are optional: a running [[cogc|COGC]] program for your industry speeds up your buildings by 25 %.

The number next to an unticked option tells you how many planets ticking it gives. *Any* in a section's header sets the section to hide as few planets as possible.

More: [Planet conditions](/help/planet_search#planet-conditions), [COGC and infrastructure](/help/planet_search#cogc-and-infrastructure).

## 3. Limit the distance {#step-3}

Fewer [[jumps]] mean shorter flights for your supply and your sales, so stay close to your exchange and your other bases.

1. Under **Distance**, tick your home exchange under **Exchanges**.
2. Logged in, you can also pick plans under **Plans from empire**: the planet must then be near them too.
3. Set **Max jumps** (0 to 30, starts at 10).

Each pick gets a jumps column. "—" means there is no jump route.

> [!tip]
> Looking for a second base next to your first? Pick its plan under **Distance** and set **Max jumps** to 2 or 3.

More: [Distance](/help/planet_search#distance).

## 4. Check for free plots {#step-4}

A planet whose [[plots]] are all taken is useless to you. Turn on **Plots** under **Columns**: it shows free plots out of the total, as FIO last saw the planet.

- A full planet shows a red ⊘ next to its name, and **Full** in the Plots column.
- **Full · ghost sites** in amber: only new players who start on that planet can still settle there.
- **plots unknown**: PRUNplanner hasn't read the planet's plots yet; check it in the game.

Full planets stay in the list; plots don't filter.

More: [Plots](/help/planet_search#plots).

## 5. Compare the results {#step-5}

Switch the view next to the heading:

- **List**: one row per planet. Click a header to sort; Shift+click another to add a tiebreaker, such as jumps first, then the most output.
- **Matrix**: every material found gets a column, shaded by output. The quickest way to spot planets with two or three useful resources.
- **Chart**: distance across, output up. The highlighted **best trade-off** planets are those no other planet beats on both. **Colour by** **COGC** shows the running programs.

What good looks like: a few planets near the top of the List that are also on the best trade-off line.

More: [List and Matrix](/help/planet_search#list-and-matrix), [Chart](/help/planet_search#chart), [Sorting](/help/planet_search#sorting).

## 6. Pin and compare up to four {#step-6}

On screens 1,024 px and wider, click ☆ on up to four planets. The tray at the bottom puts them side by side and colours the rows from best to worst: fertility, output of each searched material, jumps to your picks, and the free workers of each tier from the planet's latest [[popr|population report]].

> [!tip]
> Few free workers of a tier you need means your base will compete for them. Prefer a planet that covers your workforce.

More: [Compare](/help/planet_search#compare).

## 7. Open it as a plan {#step-7}

Click "+" on the planet's row: a new plan for that planet opens in a new tab, and your search stays as it is. In the **Chart**, double-click a dot to do the same.

To come back to the search later, **Copy link** copies the whole search (filters, sort, view and pins); **Save search** keeps the filters in this browser only.

More: [Saving and sharing](/help/planet_search#saving-and-sharing), [Base setup](/help/plan_base).
