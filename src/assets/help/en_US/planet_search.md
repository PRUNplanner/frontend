**Planet Search** finds a planet you already know, or places for new production. Every change to a filter updates the results right away.

**Name or ID.** Type part of a planet's name or natural ID (e.g. "OT-580" or "Montem") in the box at the top.

**Materials.** Each group matches *any of* or *all of* its materials, and the groups combine with *all groups* or *any group*. That covers searches like "(FEO and H2O) or (MAG and O)". Every material can have a minimum daily extraction in units per day. The number next to each option is how many planets you would get with it.

**Planet conditions.** Pick Rocky and/or Gaseous planets, and whether they must be fertile. Gravity, pressure and temperature only matter through the extra building materials they require (MGC, BL, SEA, HSE, INS, TSH). Tick the ones you are willing to ship in; planets needing any other one are hidden.

**COGC and infrastructure.** Filter by the COGC program running right now, and by Local Market, Chamber of Commerce, Warehouse, Administration Center or Shipyard.

**Distance.** Pick exchanges and/or plans from one of your empires. A planet must be within the max jumps of every pick, and each pick gets its own jumps column. AI1, CI1, IC1 and NC1 are always sortable columns.

**List and Matrix.** The List shows the searched materials as tiles next to the other resources. The Matrix gives every material found in the results its own column, searched ones first; use *Columns* to hide columns or materials.

**Sorting.** Click a column header to sort by it, click again to reverse. Shift+click another header to add it as a tiebreaker (the headers show 1, 2, 3): "NC1 jumps, then FEO /day" lists the richest FEO planets at each distance. The *Sort* menu does the same without a keyboard.

**Compare.** On larger screens, pin up to four planets with ☆ to compare them side by side. The best value in each ranked row is bold.

**Saving and sharing.** The address bar always holds the whole search, so *Copy link* shares it. "+" opens a new plan for that planet in a new tab, so your search stays where it is. *Save search* stores named searches in this browser; *Manage* renames, loads or deletes them.
